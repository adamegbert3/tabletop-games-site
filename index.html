Tabletop Games Nearby

Support

Family card, dice, board and party games for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Play with people nearby over Bluetooth, with no Wi-Fi or internet needed.

Getting help

Email austin.virgin@gmail.com and tell us your device and what happened. We reply as soon as we can.

Quick answers

  • I can't see a game nearby. Turn Bluetooth on, allow Local Network and Bluetooth when asked (Settings → Tabletop Games), and keep the host's screen open and close by.
  • A friend doesn't have the app. In the lobby, show them the QR code or tap Share link. They can join from a web browser on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
  • Keeping score for any game. Use Scorecard on the home screen. For the dice game Skunk, choose Skunk scorekeeper.
