Privacy Policy

Tabletop Games Nearby · Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

We don't collect your data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses, and why

  • Bluetooth and Local Network: to find and connect to people playing near you, and to let friends open a game from a web browser on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Games run on the host's own device; the connection is between your devices.
  • The name you type: a player name is shown to the other players in the same game so everyone knows who is who. It stays on the devices in that game. It is not stored on a server, and it isn't shared with us.
  • On your device: the app remembers things like your name and settings in its own storage on your device so you don't have to type them again. You can remove them by deleting the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Children

The app doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.
